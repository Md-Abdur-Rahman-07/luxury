A little clarity before you go

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Planning should feel as comfortable as the journey. Here are a few useful things to know.

How do I arrange a private journey?

Send a quote request with your pickup location, destination, date and guest count. Our team will discuss the details and availability with you. Your request becomes a booking only when the arrangements are confirmed.

Can you help with a wedding or a group itinerary?

Yes. Share the locations, approximate timings and number of guests through the inquiry form. We can discuss how to coordinate the travel, including multiple stops or journeys across the day.

Can I create my own Hill Country experience?

Start with our winery, brewery, dining and nightlife guides. Tell us which places interest you, and we can discuss the travel itinerary. Venue reservations, tickets and opening hours should be confirmed directly with each venue.

What if my plans change?

Email example@gmail.com with your name and journey details as soon as you can. Changes depend on availability and the terms agreed when your journey was confirmed.

Which time zone should I use for my pickup?

Use Austin local time (Central Time) when entering your pickup date and time. For an airport arrival, include your flight information in the additional details so the timing can be discussed accurately.
